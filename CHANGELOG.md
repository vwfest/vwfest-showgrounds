# What's New

## 2026-10-31
- Phone fixes: the emote bar no longer covers the chat tabs and the drive button, and the prompt and DJ chip no longer sit on top of the chat or menu.
- "New profile" starts with an empty nickname box (an old pre-filled name made a new one get cut at 14 characters).
- If the game server is asleep or down you now see what is happening and your Join click is kept, instead of "Connecting…" forever.
- Fixed the big empty boxes in the Outfit Shop, and the stage screen no longer hides the SHOW GROUNDS sign.
- Safety: stronger limits on chat, voice and DJ microphone abuse, plus moderator tools to mute, kick or ban.
- "Continue with Google" is available (test network): sign in with a Google account to get a wallet you control and can export, e.g. to show an NFT on your avatar. No card payments.
- DJ booth in front of the stage: claim the decks (queue and time limit), synced house playlist everyone hears with volume that fades away from the stage, your own volume and mute, DJ microphone to talk over the music, DJ name and now-playing screen, stage lights that pulse to the beat, and a crowd hype emote. DJs must play only music they have the rights to.
- Use an NFT you already own as your avatar: verified on-chain, shown on your chest and in a gold frame above your head, re-checked regularly (free, no burn).
- Fixed walking: forward/back and left/right were mirrored on keyboard and joystick. The Info Booth moved clear of the arcade kiosks.
- Info Booth at the entrance with this guide, always generated from the live prices, fees and odds.
- Vendor Row: claim a booth for {vendorFeeUsd} and customize it.
- Entry / Mint: sign in with Google to get a wallet, fund it, and mint your avatar without owning a wallet beforehand (card payments are not offered).
- Pay with any Solana token (USDC, BONK, JUP...) in the same transaction.
